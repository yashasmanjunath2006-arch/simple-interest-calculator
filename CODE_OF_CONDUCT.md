
# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a welcoming and respectful environment for everyone.

## Our Standards

Positive behavior includes:

- Being respectful and polite.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Helping other contributors.
- Respecting different opinions.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, and insulting others.

## Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying standards and taking appropriate action.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and community interactions.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
